Ravion gives teams and their agents fast, powerful building blocks for a faster, safer software factory: CI/CD pipelines, deploys, and Terraform automation, connected on one canvas and running in your own cloud. Start from our open standard library of modules or your own Terraform, adopt one building block at a time, and keep every change audited and reversible.

First-party production-ready modules and pipelines support AWS today, and other clouds are planned. Ravion connects your existing infrastructure, deployments, failures, logs, and metrics, so you keep your telemetry and native AWS tools instead of replacing them.

### Ship as fast as your agents write code

Run fast CI/CD pipelines on warm sandboxes with whole-disk caching, plus deploys and Terraform automation, all in your own cloud. Every change is visible and reviewable, and deploys roll back with one command, so your team can hand agents more of the work.

### Establish a production-grade AWS system

Start with production-ready modules and pipelines for web services, databases, caches, and networking, without assembling every DevOps capability yourself. Everything runs in your AWS account, and you can customize the system as your needs grow.

### Unify a fragmented AWS estate or extend your developer platform

Turn existing IaC into governed, self-service modules. Developers and AI agents provision, deploy, and operate services through Ravion while DevOps retains control of Terraform, module definitions, permissions, policies, pipelines, approvals, and the AWS account. Adopt Ravion incrementally instead of rewriting or migrating everything at once.

## What you can do

- **[Run CI/CD pipelines](/concepts/pipelines)** securely inside your own VPC, or keep your existing CI and trigger deploys from it
- **[Turn your Terraform into reusable modules](/module-definitions/definition-schema)** with typed inputs, so consuming a VPC or database is filling in a form, not reading HCL
- **[Provision infrastructure](/modules/stack)** with reviewable plans, approval gates, and every resource change tracked over time
- **[Build](/modules/build) and [deploy](/modules/deploy) any app** with blue-green rollouts, instance-level visibility, and one-command rollback
- **[Manage everything as code](/config-as-code/project-config-file)**: environments, modules, and pipelines from declarative files in your repo
- **[Let AI agents discover, plan, act, verify, and recover](/start-here)** through structured configuration, the CLI, and the API, with governed permissions and reviewable plans, and give them docs search through the [Ravion Docs MCP server](/mcp/overview)
